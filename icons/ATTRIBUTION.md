# Icon attribution

`head/`, `drive/`, `nuts/`, `washers/` and `rivets/` are generated by `generate.py` in this directory: plain
geometric silhouettes, no third-party artwork, do what you like with them.

`inserts/`, `electronics/` and everything under `halagen/` come from the [halagen](https://github.com/timmmmmmmmm/halagen)
project (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2025 Hardware Label Maker). Halagen's set is in turn based
on **Joe Jankowiak's Gridfinity bin label icons**,
https://www.printables.com/model/621771-gridfinity-bin-label-icons, used under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

`halagen/drive/*.png` were rasterised from halagen's SVG `heads/` originals at 256 px with resvg;
`halagen/head/` is halagen's `fasteners/`. `heads/deprecated` was not copied.

Drop your own PNG/JPG/BMP icons into any subfolder here (or a new one); they show up in the picker
under that folder's name. Black on white or on transparent, any size; they are thresholded to 1-bit.
